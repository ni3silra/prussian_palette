Welcome

Hi, I'm Asha 🌿

Art found me when I needed it most. What began as a quiet weekend escape — brushes, water, colour and the particular hush of an empty afternoon — became something I simply could not put down.

Now I paint nature, stillness and the beauty of everywhere I wander. From my home in Frankfurt — for homes that breathe. I work in watercolour and mixed media, drawn always to the palette that first undid me: Prussian Blue, Sage, the warmth of old paper.

My paintings are quiet. They don't ask to be looked at — they wait to be noticed. And then they stay.

— Asha
