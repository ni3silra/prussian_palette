Who is Asha?

"Hi, I'm Asha"

Art found me when I needed it most. Now I paint nature, stillness, and the beauty of everywhere I wander — from my home in Frankfurt — for homes that breathe. My work blends abstract organic forms with deep Prussian and lunar tones, designed to bring a calm, breathing presence to your living spaces.
